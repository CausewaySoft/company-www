If you're a small or micro business in Northern Ireland, the **Digital Transformation Flexible Fund (DTFF)** could be your gateway to cutting-edge innovation.

With funding between **£5,000 and £20,000**, you can invest in technologies such as:

- **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning**  
- **Internet of Things (IoT)**  
- **Smart Environments & Simulation**  
- **Robotics & Additive Manufacturing**  
- **Big Data & Analytics**  
- **Immersive Technologies (AR/VR/MR/Haptics)**  
- **Blockchain & System Integration**

---

## Funding Details

This is a **capital grant**, covering **up to 70%** of your project costs, with a **30% match funding** requirement.  
**Note:** DTFF does **not** cover ongoing maintenance or leasing costs - it’s intended for **capital investment** only.  
[Read official applicant guidance (PDF)](https://dtff.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/2024-Apr-DTFF-Applicant-Guidance-Final.pdf)

---

## Eligibility Criteria

To qualify, your business must:

- Be located in one of Northern Ireland's 11 council areas  
- Employ between 1 and 49 people (micro or small business)  
- Be VAT registered (or exempt)  
- Have been trading for at least 12 months  
- Possess a Digital Transformation Plan or equivalent  
- Not have received over €300,000 in State Aid/Subsidy over the past 3 years
