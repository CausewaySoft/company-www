## What is the Digital Transformation Flexible Fund (DTFF)?

The **DTFF grant** gives Northern Ireland SMEs up to **£20,000** to invest in technology and software that accelerates growth and efficiency.  

- Covers up to 70% of project costs  
- Eligible for businesses trading 12+ months with 10–249 employees  
- Supports AI, IoT, workflow automation, analytics dashboards, AR/VR, robotics, blockchain, and more  

**Why this matters:** Most small and micro businesses struggle with manual processes and outdated systems. DTFF funding allows you to implement **modern technology solutions without shouldering the full cost.**
